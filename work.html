What these projects show

I work with existing small businesses, startups, independent developers and game studios. Each case study explains a specific design brief, the visual decisions behind the work and the files or applications included. Use the examples to assess whether the approach fits your project.

JacyJo Photography’s brand identity connects an editorial wordmark with supporting marks, typography, a pattern and printed materials. The social media design collection shows Facebook covers and a coordinated nonprofit campaign, where the challenge is keeping different messages visually connected.

For game teams, Fuse a Brainrot’s Roblox interface brings its HUD, inventory, shop and rewards into one system. Ascendant Link’s RPG interface extends that thinking across character, equipment, crafting and quest screens. These are examples of visual design scope; scripting and implementation are agreed separately.

A complete case study can cover more than a starting package. When enquiring, mention the project you relate to, your priority deliverables and your timeline so we can define an appropriate scope.

Logo Design

Custom logo design for small businesses, startups and creators who need a distinctive mark for their website, social profiles and print materials. Work directly with Rameez Ahmed, the designer behind Evoke Creative Creations.

Discuss your projectView starting price
The problem we solve

Your business needs a recognizable mark that works beyond a mockup.

A focused logo direction with practical files for your website, social profiles and print.

Rameez reviews your audience, reference material and practical requirements, then develops the visual direction in Photoshop and Illustrator. Your feedback shapes the final design and the agreed files.

Is this the right service for your project?

A logo gives people a recognizable mark to associate with your business. This service focuses on that mark; a full brand identity adds a wider color, typography and application system.

Designed by Rameez Ahmed at Evoke Creative Creations, an independent studio with 9+ years of design experience. Explore the portfolio to see the work before you enquire.

What we can design

A custom logo direction

A focused visual concept developed around your business name, audience and references.

Refinement and variations

Refine the chosen direction for legibility and practical use. Agree any secondary marks or icon variations in the brief.

Files for actual use

Final SVG, transparent PNG and JPG files for the agreed logo. Editable source-file requirements are confirmed in your quote.

A clear handoff

Confirm the sizes and backgrounds needed for your website, profile image or printed materials.

From brief to final files

  1. Understand. Define the problem, audience and practical requirements. Review questions and references together, then confirm the scope and quote.
  2. Design. Rameez develops a focused visual direction around the agreed problem and requirements.
  3. Refine. Review the design against the brief and practical use cases, then refine it through the included feedback rounds.
  4. Delivery. Prepare the approved files for the formats and placements agreed at the start.

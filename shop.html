Files & usage

Before you download.

Which files do I get?

The free ZIP includes a Roblox Studio Shop and HUD demo plus nine SVG and PNG assets. The full pack adds Passes, Inventory, Upgrades and Daily Rewards in both themes, plus the artwork library, colour tokens and asset license.

Can I use this in Roblox Studio?

Open the .rbxlx demo place in Roblox Studio or insert the .rbxmx ScreenGui into StarterGui in your project. The demo uses temporary data. For a released game, connect the interface to your own game logic.

Can I use it in a commercial game?

The included license allows you to use and modify the assets in personal, commercial and client games. You can distribute them as part of a finished game. Standalone resale or redistribution of the assets is excluded.

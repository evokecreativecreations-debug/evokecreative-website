Working together

From a useful conversation to usable files.

  1. Understand what needs to change. We discuss your audience, current design, references and the problem you want to solve. For a game, that includes the screens and actions players need to understand.
  2. Agree the scope. Deliverables, price, timeline, file formats and revision rounds are confirmed before design starts. Larger identity systems and multi-screen interfaces receive a custom quote.
  3. Develop and refine the direction. I design around the agreed brief, explain the choices and work through your consolidated feedback. The focus stays on how the design will be used.
  4. Prepare the handoff. Approved artwork is organized into the agreed formats. A brand project might need logo exports and a guide; a UI project might need layered PSD files and PNG assets for the developer.

Research supports the creative work.

I use AI during research to organize briefs, explore audience questions and review reference material. It helps surface questions worth resolving before committing to a visual direction.
